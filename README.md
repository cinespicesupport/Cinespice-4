# CineSpice

**Your Telegram video library, made for watching.**

CineSpice brings videos from your selected Telegram channels and groups into one
place on iPhone, iPad and Android. Browse your library, find something to watch,
and play it directly in the app.

## Browse your way

- Choose the channels and groups you want in your library.
- Search by title or filename across your selected sources, or within one channel.
- Keep Favorites and a Watchlist for videos you want to return to.
- Pick up where you left off with Continue Watching and watch history.
- Use quick Search and Top shortcuts when browsing long lists.
- Choose from light and dark themes.

Your library comes from the Telegram sources you select and can access with your
account. CineSpice does not provide a separate movie catalogue.

## Watch comfortably

- Stream without waiting for the whole file to download.
- Resume a video or start again from the beginning.
- Seek with the timeline, ten-second controls or a double tap.
- Select audio and subtitle tracks, or import external tracks.
- Adjust subtitle size, audio/subtitle timing and playback speed.
- Choose picture sizing and orientation, and lock controls while watching.

Playback uses VLC. Format, codec and subtitle support depend on the file;
some videos may need a complete download before they can play.

## Download and keep a copy

Download videos to watch from the app, pause a transfer and resume it later.
Resuming reuses partial data while it remains in the app's cache. Completed
downloads have a **Save to device** button so you can keep a copy in your chosen
Files or storage location.

Keep CineSpice open while downloading. Clearing the cache or signing out can
remove cached media, including partial downloads. Copies saved to your device
remain separate from the app's cache.

## Member access

Sign in with your Telegram account, then tap **Activate**. The CineSpice bot checks
that your account belongs to the paid group. If it does not, contact support.
Joining the updates channel is optional.

Activation is saved for your account and app installation. Closing the app or a
temporary bot outage does not require another activation. Signing out or reinstalling
requires activation again. Activating on another installation moves access there;
the previous installation will be blocked when it can verify the change. Leaving
the paid group or an administrator revoking access also blocks access once verified.

## Announcements and updates

Announcements appear as compact banners inside CineSpice. Tap a banner to read
the full message, photo or animation, then close it when you are done.

The **Notifications** button beside **Channels** opens announcement history.
Dismissing a banner keeps its message in history until an administrator clears it.
The bot also delivers announcements in Telegram, where normal Telegram
notifications follow your notification settings.

## Get started

1. Install the CineSpice build provided for your device.
2. Sign in with your Telegram phone number and complete the requested verification.
3. Tap **Activate** and follow the bot's membership instructions.
4. Open **Channels**, choose your sources, and browse or search for a video.
5. Tap a video to watch, or choose **Download** to keep it in the app.

Supported devices: **iPhone and iPad with iOS 16 or later**, and **Android 8.0 or
later**. An internet connection is needed for sign-in, activation, streaming and
downloading.

## Your account and library

Your channel selections, Favorites, Watchlist and viewing progress are stored on
your device for your Telegram account. Settings let you manage downloaded media,
artwork and watch history, or sign out. Clearing the app's cache does not delete
messages from Telegram.

Never share your Telegram login codes or two-step verification password.

## Need help?

Open the CineSpice bot in Telegram and send **/help** for the support group and
contact buttons. Send **/start** for a short introduction and links to the chat
group, contact and updates channel.
